# Crystal v0.8.4 — Novel Behavior / Unknown Interaction Engine

Crystal now explicitly separates two research worlds.

## Known Weakness Layer

Known vulnerabilities and recurring patterns are a baseline, regression suite,
and comparison reference. They are not the innovation target.

## Novel Behavior Layer

Crystal explores behavior without requiring a named vulnerability class.

It examines:

- state interactions
- sequence novelty
- invariant deltas
- cross-component behavior
- unexpected combinations of otherwise ordinary operations

## Novelty Score

Novelty is a **research-priority metric**, never a vulnerability-confidence
metric.

A highly novel behavior can still be benign.

## Unknown Interaction Discovery

Candidate behaviors that do not closely resemble known operation templates are
prioritized for deeper validation.

## Hard boundary

Novelty does not bypass the finding gate.

`NOVEL` != `VULNERABLE` != `CONFIRMED`.

The only externally confirmed finding remains one that passes every concrete
proof gate.
